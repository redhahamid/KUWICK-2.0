Already decided by you:

- Doctor or staff comes from the employee record, not the account role. The
  owner can be a doctor. (Being built now.)
- Overtime basis is a clinic setting with three choices:
  1. **Employment Act (default):** hours beyond the normal daily hours (per
     shift, at most 8 a day), plus hours beyond the weekly normal not already
     counted.
  2. **Weekly only:** the clinic's current practice, shown with a warning that

     12 hours.
  Doctors' locum hours stay weekly.

## Overtime and locum pay (section C)

1. **Who gets overtime.** The RM4,000 wage limit is a setting, but it can only
   be raised (to give overtime to more staff), never lowered below the Act's
   RM4,000. Recommended: **yes**.
2. **Public holiday rate.** Each holiday has its own multiplier, but for staff
   under the Act it cannot be set below the Act (2 days' pay for the day, 3×
   for hours beyond the normal day). Doctors can be 1×. Recommended: **yes**.
3. **The roster is the approval.** With the default paid-hours rule (a), hours
   on the published roster above the weekly normal are overtime without any
   form. Only hours worked beyond the roster need a manager's approval.
   Recommended: **yes**.
4. **Doctor night shift.** Paid as a fixed amount per night shift (default
   RM350), set per doctor or per night-shift template, whatever its length.
   A shorter or longer night is still one shift. Recommended: **yes**.
5. **When the week crosses two months.** The whole week's overtime or locum
   hours belong to the month the week ends in, and are paid in the payroll
   after that month. Recommended: **yes**.

## Leave (section D)

6. **Statutory floors.** Default leave types start at the Act's minimums
   (e.g. annual 8/12/16 days by service, sick 14/18/22, maternity 98 days,
   paternity 7 days). A clinic can give more, never less, to staff under the
   Act. Recommended: **yes**.
7. **First-year accrual.** The "1 day per N days worked, up to a cap" rule is
   a setting. Kuwick warns if it would give less than the Act's pro-rated
   minimum. Recommended: **yes**.
8. **Replacement day not taken.** A replacement day for working a public
   holiday expires after a set time (default 90 days). If it expires unused it
   is paid out at the holiday rate, not lost, because the Act requires the
   holiday to be given. Recommended: **yes**.

## Claims (section E)

9. **Over the daily km cap.** A mileage claim over the cap (e.g. 50 km a day)
   is paid up to the cap, and the claim shows what was not paid and why.
   Recommended: **yes**.
10. **Tax treatment of claims.** Claims stay "not taxable" as today, but
    marked "to confirm with your tax adviser" until someone confirms it.
    Kuwick does not decide tax treatment. Recommended: **yes**.

## Medical benefit (section F)

11. **Pro-rating for new staff.** A new joiner's yearly limit is the remaining
    whole months ÷ 12 × the limit, to the sen. Recommended: **yes**.
12. **Bill bigger than what is left.** When a counter bill is charged to the
    benefit and the balance is short, the benefit pays what is left and the
    rest is paid at the counter as usual. Recommended: **yes**.
13. **Doctor's approval.** The doctor who saw the patient confirms the charge
    to the benefit. Their name and the time are kept on the bill.
    Recommended: **yes**.

## QR attendance (section B), decided for you unless you say otherwise

These do not block anything; I am building them as below.

- **Outside the clinic location:** a setting per branch, Off, Flag (default)
  or Refuse. Flag records the scan and shows it to HR.
- **PIN fallback:** each person sets a 4–6 digit PIN in My Kuwick. A kiosk PIN
  entry is always flagged for HR to see.
- **QR lifetime:** the code changes every 30 seconds (a setting), and a code
  is accepted only in its own 30 seconds plus a few seconds' grace for a slow
  phone.
