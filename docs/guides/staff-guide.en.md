# My Kuwick — guide for staff

Everything you do in My Kuwick on your phone. One action per step. "You see"
says what the screen shows after it. Sign in with the email and password your
clinic gave you.

## 1. Clock in and out with the QR code

1. Open **My Kuwick**. On **Home**, press **Scan to clock in**.
   You see the camera.
2. Point the phone at the QR code on the clinic screen.
   You see "Clocked in at 08:02". Home shows "On duty since 08:02".
3. At the end of the shift, press **Scan to clock out** and scan again.
   You see "Clocked out at 17:05".
4. Scanned twice by mistake? Kuwick ignores a second scan within one minute
   and says "Already recorded a moment ago".

## 2. Clock in with your PIN (when the phone cannot scan)

1. Once, set your PIN: **Attendance → Kiosk PIN**, type 4 to 6 digits, press **Save PIN**.
   You see "PIN saved."
2. At the clinic screen, press **Use PIN instead**.
3. Pick your name and type your PIN.
   You see your clock-in recorded. HR sees it marked *PIN at kiosk*.

## 3. See your roster

1. Open **Attendance**.
   You see **My roster**: your published shifts for the month.
2. Press **‹** or **›** to see another month.
   No shifts? The month is not published yet. Ask whoever manages the roster.

## 4. Apply for leave

1. Open **Leave**.
   You see your balances at the top, e.g. "14 Annual leave left".
2. Choose the **Type**, **From** and **To** dates, and *Half day* if needed.
   You see how many days it takes and your balance after it.
3. For medical leave, press **Take a photo or choose a file** and photograph your MC.
4. Press **Submit request**.
   You see "Sent to your manager." The request shows under **My requests** as *Waiting*.
5. When your manager decides, the request shows *Approved* or *Not approved*.

## 5. Cancel a leave request

1. Open **Leave**. Under **My requests**, find the request marked *Waiting*.
2. Press **Cancel request**, then **Yes, cancel it**.
   You see "Leave request cancelled." It is gone from the list.
   A request already approved can only be changed by your manager.

## 6. Submit a claim with a receipt

1. Open **Claims**. Choose the **Type** and the date.
2. Mileage: type **From**, **To** and the **Distance** in km.
   You see the amount worked out for you.
   Other claims: type the **Amount**.
3. Type the **Purpose**.
4. Press **Take a photo of the receipt or choose a file**, then take the photo.
   You see the photo under the box.
5. Press **Submit claim**.
   You see "Sent to your manager". The claim shows under **My claims** as *Waiting*.
6. Changed your mind while it waits? Press **Withdraw** on the claim.
   When it is approved it shows *Approved*, and *Paid* once paid with your salary.

## 7. Payslips

1. Open **Payslips**.
   You see every paid month with your net pay.
2. Press **Lines** to see what made up the month.
3. Press **PDF** to download the payslip.
   The latest payslip is also on **Home** under *Latest payslip*.

## 8. Medical benefit balance

1. Open **Claims** and scroll to **Medical benefit**.
   You see how much you have used this year out of your limit.

## 9. Ask for an attendance fix

1. Open **Attendance** and find the day in the month list.
2. Press **Ask for a fix** under the date.
3. Type the right clock-in and/or clock-out time and what happened.
4. Press **Send to my manager**.
   You see the day marked "Fix asked for · waiting for your manager".
   The original time stays on the record; your manager decides.

## 10. Your details and bank account

1. Open **Profile**. Under **Contact**, press **Edit**.
2. Change your phone, email, address or emergency contact. Press **Save**.
   You see "Your details are saved."
3. New bank account: under **Bank account**, press **Change**.
4. Type the bank, account number and name on the account.
5. Press **Take a photo or choose a file** and photograph the top of a bank
   statement or your bank card.
6. Press **Send to HR**.
   You see "Waiting for HR". Your pay keeps going to the old account until HR approves.
7. When HR approves, **Pay goes to** shows the new account.
