# Kuwick HR — guide for the owner and HR

From a brand-new clinic to the year-end EA forms, in the order you will meet
them. One action per step. "You see" says what the screen shows after it.
Every number (hours, rates, limits, days) is your clinic's own setting: you
change it in Settings.

## 1. First-time setup

1. Open **HR**. While setup is unfinished, HR opens on **Set up payroll**:
   eight short steps with a counter such as "3/8". Do them in any order.
   When a step opens another page, the eight steps stay pinned on the left;
   press a step to go back, or **Leave setup** to see the normal HR menu.
2. **Company & statutory numbers**: type the company name, registration
   number, EPF, SOCSO and LHDN (E) numbers, the company phone and address
   (the postcode fills the town and state), and who signs the EA form and
   their position. Choose the **HRD Corp registration** ("Not registered" is
   an answer too) and upload the logo for payslips. Press **Save & next**.
   You see the step turn green. "Missing" on the Company page now matches it.
3. **Company bank account**: choose the bank, type the account name and number.
   You see "On file". The number is never shown again, only its last four digits.
4. **Pay rules**: check how a part month, overtime and approval work.
   Under **Defaults for new staff** set days a week, probation length, your
   department list and what employee numbers start with (E gives E001,
   E002…).
5. **Payroll items**: press **Add default items** (right in the step). On
   the Payroll items page, items only some clinics pay (house call, panel
   claim, procedure incentives) are then listed under **Optional items**:
   press **Add** beside the ones you use. Each item says when it is paid
   (e.g. the 30th, or the 15th of the next month). Click a row to change it.
6. **Public holidays**: choose your state, untick any day you do not
   observe, press **Add … holidays**. Dates marked *check* can move; confirm
   them against the official list. A holiday on the weekly rest day brings
   its replacement day (cuti ganti), also marked *check*. Kuwick remembers
   your state. **Edit the calendar** opens the Public holidays page.
7. **Opening balances**: did you pay staff this year in another system?
   *No* finishes the step. *Yes* opens Opening balances to bring in each
   person's pay so far, so their tax and EA form are right.
8. **Employees**: press **Add your first employee** (section 2).
9. **Statutory rates**: see which EPF, SOCSO and EIS tables are used (date
   and source) and press **Acknowledge**.
   You see **Setup complete** and **Run your first payroll →**. From now on
   HR opens on Payroll.

## 2. Employees

1. Open **HR → Employees** and press **+ Add employee**.
2. Type the name, branch, position, start date and type of employment. Press **Save**.
   The employee number is filled in with the next one (E001, E002…); change
   it if you need to. A new person is put on the clinic's leave policy
   (Kuwick makes "Standard leave" from your leave types if you have none:
   annual leave 8 days a year, pro-rated in the year they join).
   You see the new record with coloured dots for what is still missing.
   *Account: No login* means you did not give them a login.
   **Probation until** is filled from your default; the Employees page
   reminds you a month before it ends. The **Review** step says whether
   payroll can pay them, using the same check payroll uses.
3. On the record, press **Edit** on each part and fill in IC or passport,
   income tax number, EPF and SOCSO numbers, bank account and salary.
   You see the dots turn green. "Complete" means payroll can pay them.
4. Doctors: set **Doctor or staff** to *Doctor*. They get clinical access
   (consultations, notes, MCs). To stop it, set **Clinical access** to
   *No clinical access*.
5. Many people at once: press **Add several** and paste or upload the list.
   You see a check of every row before anything is saved.
6. Somebody leaves: open **⋯ → Mark as resigned** and type the last day.
   You see them under *Resigned*. Their payslips stay.
7. A yellow notice "new bank account waiting" appears when staff ask for a
   new account in My Kuwick. Press **Check now**, compare the photo with the
   new account, then press **Approve**, or type a reason and press **Not approve**.
   You see the notice go when none are left.

## 3. Shifts and roster

1. Open **Settings → Shifts** and add your shift templates (name, start, end,
   break, night shift or not).
   You see them listed with their hours.
2. Open **HR → Roster** and press your branch's name.
   You see the month with every employee of the branch, doctors and staff.
3. Select a day (or several days) in a person's row and press **Add shift**.
   You see the shift in the cells. External locums are added by name.
4. When the month is right, press **Publish**.
   You see "Published to My Kuwick." Staff now see their shifts.
   The published roster is the approval for normal hours.

## 4. QR attendance

1. On the clinic tablet, open **HR → Attendance** and press **Open kiosk screen**
   (choose the branch first). Leave the screen open.
   You see a large QR code that changes every half minute.
2. Staff press **Scan to clock in** in My Kuwick and point the phone at it.
   They see "Clocked in at 08:02".
3. A phone that cannot scan: on the kiosk the person presses **Use PIN instead**,
   picks their name and types their PIN.
   You see the punch marked *PIN at kiosk* on the Attendance page.
4. On **HR → Attendance**, choose **Exceptions only**.
   You see missed scans, late arrivals, early leaving and scans away from the clinic.
5. A staff "fix" request: open it, check, press **Approve** or **Reject**.
   You see the original punch kept and the corrected time beside it.

## 5. Leave

1. **Settings → Attendance & leave → Leave types**: check days a year, paid or
   unpaid, and whether a document is needed.
2. Leave taken before Kuwick: **HR → Leave → Import opening balances**.
   You see each person's balance updated.
3. Requests arrive in **HR → Leave** under *Waiting for decision*. Open one and
   press **Approve** or **Reject**.
   You see the staff member's balance change. Unpaid leave comes off pay in
   that month's payroll.

## 6. Claims

1. **Settings → Attendance & leave → Claim types**: set the mileage rate per km,
   the daily km cap and which types need a receipt.
2. Claims arrive in **HR → Claims** under *Waiting for decision*. Open one, look
   at the receipt photo, press **Approve** or **Reject**.
   You see approved claims "into Oct payroll": they are paid with the next payroll.

## 7. Medical benefit

1. Open **HR → Employees → Medical benefits** and press **Benefit settings**.
   Set the yearly limit, the joining-month cut-off day and the low-balance alert.
2. Press **Open 2026 accounts** (the year shown).
   You see one account per employee; joiners get a part-year amount.
3. Click a person and use **Record use** with the date, amount and what it was for.
   You see the balance go down, and *Low* or *Overdrawn* when it runs out.

## 8. Monthly payroll

1. Open **HR → Payroll**, pick the month and press **Start** beside your branch.
   You see the **Prepare** step: one row per employee.
2. Prepare: type allowances, overtime and deductions in the white cells, or
   press **Import items (CSV)**.
   You see grey cells already filled from records (salary, unpaid leave, claims).
3. After any change, press **Work the month out again**. Then open **Review**.
   You see what blocks the month (red), what is worth a look (amber) and the totals.
4. Fix each red item, usually a missing record, and press **Work the month out again**.
   A month earlier in the year with no payroll shows **Run <month> payroll →**.
5. **Approve**: read the summary and press **Approve the month**. An amber
   line names anybody without an EPF or income tax number: you can still
   approve, but the EPF and PCB filings will be missing their number.
   You see the month locked and payslips made.
6. **Pay**: download the bank listing, pay at your bank's website, then press
   **Record the transfer** with the date and bank reference.
   You see the month marked *Paid*. Staff see their payslip in My Kuwick.
7. Statutory filing: on **Pay** (or **Reports → To send**) download each CSV
   (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, HRD), upload it on the authority's website, then
   press **Record filing** with their reference.
   You see each one marked *Filed* with its reference.

## 9. Year end: EA forms and Borang E

1. After December is approved, open **HR → Reports** and press **Open** beside **EA forms**.
   You see **Year-end forms**: a check list, the EA forms and Borang E.
2. Read **Check before you print**. Fix each name listed, usually a missing
   income tax number or the employer's E number, on the employee or company record.
   You see the list get shorter. The forms update by themselves.
3. Press **Download all** to get every EA form, one page per person.
   Give each employee theirs **by the end of February**.
4. Under **Borang E**, check the totals and press **Employee list (C.P.8D)**.
   File Borang E on LHDN e-Filing **by 31 March**.
5. Before printing, check the **EA form box** of any unusual payroll item in
   **Settings → Payroll items**. When unsure, ask your tax adviser.
6. The officer who signs and their position (e.g. PENGARAH) print on every
   form: set them once in **Settings → Company & statutory**. The date
   printed is 31 December unless you choose another in **Date on the forms**.
