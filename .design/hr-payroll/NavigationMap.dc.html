Two doors, on purpose
The same capability, reached differently by two different people.
The clinic owner, once a month
Opens Payroll, presses Run payroll, answers whatever Needs attention says, reads three numbers, finalises. Never sees a component, a policy or a rate table.
The accountant or Kuwick specialist, at go-live
Opens HR settings, works the six-step setup checklist, says what each kind of pay counts towards, checks the statutory tables against KWSP and PERKESO, then leaves.
Everything the specialist configures is what makes the owner's monthly run boring. That is the whole design.
