KUWICK HR & PAYROLL — PROPOSED
Where payroll figures come from
Five chains that already exist in Kuwick’s data. The design’s job is to surface them, never to duplicate them.
Hours become pay without anybody typing them twice
A generic payroll product asks you to key in overtime. Kuwick already knows: the shift was rostered, the person clocked against it, and the hours above the shift are overtime waiting on an approval.
Roster
The shift that was planned. One roster, shared with Appointments.
Attendance
Clocked in and out against that shift.
Overtime
Hours above the shift, and whether a manager approved them.
Payroll · Earnings
Approved hours only. Unapproved ones are shown and not paid.
Leave reaches payroll as money, not as a form to re-enter
Unpaid leave is the only kind that changes pay, and it changes it by the clinic’s own proration rule rather than a guess.
Leave request
Applied for and approved in Leave.
Unpaid days
Only unpaid leave affects pay.
Payroll · Deductions
Priced on the clinic’s daily rate.
What a doctor generated becomes what a doctor is paid
This is the connection a generic payroll product cannot make at all: it has no idea which procedures a doctor performed.
Consultations & procedures
Recorded in the clinical workspace as work happens.
Doctor incentives
The clinic’s own rule, applied to that month.
Payroll · Earnings
Appears as an incentive line on the payslip.
A staff claim is approved once and paid once
Reimbursements are approved by a manager in Claims, then ride the next payroll rather than leaving petty cash.
Staff claim
Submitted with a receipt.
Approval
By the manager, with the reason on record.
Payroll · Earnings
Paid with salary, taxed correctly for its kind.
The clinic’s wage bill lands in Finance by itself
Payroll is the largest cost most clinics have, and it is the one most often missing from their own profitability view.
Finalised payroll
Locked and snapshotted.
Employer cost
Pay plus the clinic’s EPF, SOCSO and EIS.
Finance
Monthly cost, and the statutory still owed.
What this means for the design
No re-entry, anywhere
Steps 2 and 3 of the run show figures and link back to where they were recorded. Payroll never becomes a second place a person’s hours live.
Fix it at the source
“2 hours overtime need approval” opens the timesheet, not a payroll field. Correcting it in payroll would leave the two disagreeing with nothing to say which is right.
The clinic-specific edge
Roster, procedures and panel work are things a clinic system knows and a payroll product never will. That is where Kuwick beats a generic tool, not on the payslip layout.
