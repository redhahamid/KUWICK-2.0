# Payroll A–Z: a guide for owners and HR

This guide follows one new clinic through its first two payroll months: from the owner's first login to a Borang EA. Every picture is a real screen from Kuwick's staging site, taken on 3 November 2026 while the walk-through below was carried out.

The yellow note "Payroll statutory setup is not yet production-ready" on some pictures belongs to the staging site, which is for testing; a live clinic does not see it.

The clinic is a demo: **Klinik Panduan Sdn Bhd** in Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor. Its EPF, SOCSO and LHDN numbers are made up. The three people are made up too: **Dr Amin Contoh** (doctor, monthly salary), **Siti Contoh** (staff nurse, monthly salary, joined in the middle of October) and **Dr Lim Contoh** (an outside locum paid by the session).

Each step has one picture, what to do, and **Check:** what to look at before you go on. Steps marked ⚠ had a problem during the walk-through; they are listed under "Issues found" at the end.

Contents:

## A. First login and the Set up payroll wizard

### A1. Log in

![The login page](img/payroll-a-z/a01-login.webp)

Open Kuwick and log in with the owner email and password your Kuwick contact gave you.

**Check:** you land on Kuwick's home with your clinic's name at the top right.

### A2. HR opens on "Set up payroll"

![HR opens on the set-up wizard](img/payroll-a-z/a02-hr-opens-on-setup.webp)

Press **HR**. A new clinic lands on **Set up payroll**: eight short steps, in any order. The ring at the top counts the steps left.

**Check:** each step on the left shows an orange note until it is done.

### A3. Step 1: company and statutory numbers

![Step 1: company and statutory numbers](img/payroll-a-z/a03-company.webp)

Type the company name as on the SSM certificate, the registration number, and the EPF, SOCSO and LHDN (E) employer numbers. Add the phone, the address (the postcode fills the town and state), the officer who signs Borang EA, and your HRD Corp class. Press **Save & next**.

**Check:** these print on every payslip and on Borang EA, so check each digit against your letters from KWSP, PERKESO and LHDN.

### A4. Step 2: company bank account

![Step 2: the company bank account](img/payroll-a-z/a04-bank.webp)

Choose the bank salaries are paid from and type the account number.

**Check:** the account number matches your bank statement.

### A5. Step 3: pay rules

![Step 3: pay rules](img/payroll-a-z/a05-pay-rules.webp)

This shows how a part month, overtime and approval work. The defaults suit most clinics; **Open Pay rules** changes them.

**Check:** "Approved by" is who may approve a month. ⚠ See issue 4.

### A6. Step 4: payroll items

![Step 4: payroll items](img/payroll-a-z/a06-items.webp)

Press **Add default items**: basic pay, overtime, claims, unpaid leave and the usual allowances and deductions, with the date each is paid.

**Check:** the step shows the number of items in use and the pay dates (here the 30th for salary, the 15th for claims).

### A7. Step 5: public holidays

![Step 5: public holidays for Selangor](img/payroll-a-z/a07-holidays.webp)

Choose the clinic's state. Kuwick lists the federal and state holidays for the year; untick any day the clinic does not observe, then press **Add … holidays**.

**Check:** dates marked "check" (moon sighting, replacement days) against the official list.

### A8. Step 6: opening balances

![Step 6: opening balances](img/payroll-a-z/a08-opening.webp)

If Kuwick is your first payroll this year, press **No**. If you paid staff earlier this year in another system, press **Yes** and enter each person's totals so monthly tax (PCB) comes out right.

**Check:** a clinic that switches mid-year must answer Yes, or PCB will be too low.

### A9. Step 7: employees

![Step 7: employees](img/payroll-a-z/a09-employees-step.webp)

This step is done when there is somebody to pay. **Add your first employee** opens the form in part B.

**Check:** the count goes up as you add people.

### A10. Step 8: statutory rates

![Step 8: statutory rates](img/payroll-a-z/a10-rates.webp)

These are the official EPF, SOCSO and EIS tables Kuwick uses, with the date each came into force. Press **Acknowledge · next**.

**Check:** when a new table comes into force, Kuwick asks you to acknowledge it again.

## Issues found

During the walk-through (practice runs on a local copy, then the staging run) these steps were broken, confusing or failed. Each says what was seen and what was done.

1. **Broken, fixed (D6).** HR could not record a clock-in for a day with no punch: saving asked for a reason, but that form has no reason box. Fixed in this batch: the reason is asked for only when an existing punch is changed.
2. **Wording, fixed (E4).** The leave review said "Annual leave leave". Fixed.
3. **Not supported (B12, C7).** Payroll pays monthly salaries only. A locum paid by the hour or session cannot be paid through payroll, and no screen marks a person as a contractor. (Kuwick does have a **Locum sessions** page that pays contractors through Finance, but it only accepts people marked as contractors, so it cannot be reached yet.) The way that works: put outside locums on the roster by name and pay them by payment voucher through Finance.
4. **Confusing (A5, G5).** The wizard says "Approved by: Somebody other than the preparer", yet the owner approved alone. This is allowed by the setting "owner may approve their own run" (on by default, for one-person clinics), but the wizard does not say so. Fixed on 5 November: the wizard now says who really approves (for example "The owner alone (nobody else can approve yet)").
5. **Confusing (G5).** After the missing tax number was acknowledged, the Approve checklist reads "EPF and income tax numbers on file — Everyone's numbers are on file", though the nurse still has none. Fixed on 5 November: it now reads "Missing, acknowledged: …".
6. **Confusing (D9).** On Attendance, the cards read "On time today" and "OT today" even when another day is shown.
7. **Confusing (C4–C10).** The roster lists the owner's own account as a row, although the owner has no HR record and is not paid.
8. **Question (G11).** Kuwick let November be approved on 3 November, before most of the month had happened. The only sign was "1 day has no clock-in or clock-out". A warning such as "this month has not ended" may help. Fixed on 5 November: the Approve step warns (does not block) while days of the month are still to come.
9. **Wording (A5).** The Pay rules summary reads "overtime on Days in the month", which is hard to follow.
10. **Looks wrong, is right (G18, H2).** PCB is RM 0.00 for a doctor on RM 9,000 a month. He joined in October, and HASiL's computerised method counts only the pay of this calendar year (RM 15,030 chargeable after reliefs), so no tax is due yet. **How this was worked out** on the person's month shows the figures.
